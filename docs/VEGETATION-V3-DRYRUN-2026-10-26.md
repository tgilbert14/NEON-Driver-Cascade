# Vegetation contract v3 dry run (2026-10-26)

State: **DRY RUN / NO DRIVER BYTE CHANGE / EVIDENCE FOR A LATER cass AND hk DISPOSITION**

## What was run

`scripts/dryrun_vegetation_v3_adapter.R` (outside the build surface, writes nothing under `data/`)
runs the production `ann_veg()` and `ann_veg_events()`, parsed out of `scripts/build_cascade.R`, with
`R/source_adapters.R` from this branch, for every site in the committed `data/cascade.rds` site_meta.
It reads three Vegetation Structure checkouts:

| role | commit | contract the bundles carry |
|---|---|---|
| pinned (the committed family's source) | `5e73e0d` | none: the legacy one-row-per-plot shape |
| Vegetation `main` | `ac3a2b1` | `NEON-VST-DP1.10098.001-v2` |
| PR #19 head | `9340869` | `NEON-VST-DP1.10098.001-v3` |

Command (from the repository root):

```
Rscript --vanilla scripts/dryrun_vegetation_v3_adapter.R <report.md> \
  pinned=<veg checkout @5e73e0d> main=<veg checkout @ac3a2b1> v3=<veg checkout @9340869>
```

Rulings applied: V3-1 to V3-6 as given by the lead on 2026-10-26, on top of R1 to R7
(`docs/BUILD-TEST-HANDOFF.md`, 2026-10-21). In short: contract v3 resolves to its own shape and still
takes the plot-event path; `supported_legacy_schema` takes exactly the v2 vintage outcome;
`held_snapshot_event_mismatch` is excluded; a `dataCollected` token other than blank or
`allGrowthForms` never reaches selection, and where the sibling reads that channel complete the hold
is labelled `excl_partial_hold_sibling_complete`; the sibling's `superseded_in_event` and
`duplicate_of_uid` flags are counted, not applied.

The sibling-complete counts and the two row-flag counts below are evidence for a later cass and hk
disposition. They change no Driver byte: they are not columns of `annual` or `site_meta`.

## Findings

1. **The committed family is legacy, not v2.** Commit `5e73e0d` carries no contract element, so check
   (a) exercises the legacy path and the shape dispatch, not the v2 event path. Check (a2) was added
   to cover the v2 path: on Vegetation `main` the adapter at `32175d1` and this branch give identical
   values on every `veg_*` column.
2. **Check (a).** 28 of 32 `veg_*` columns are identical to the committed site_meta. The four
   floating-point columns (`veg_ba_ha`, `veg_ba_se`, `veg_class_tree_ba_ha`, `veg_class_shrub_ba_ha`)
   differ in the last bits, at most 1.5e-14 relative, and the unchanged adapter at `32175d1` gives
   the same bits as this branch on the same input. The difference is the build machine (the family
   was summed on the CI runner; this run is on Apple silicon, whose R `sum()` has no extended-precision
   accumulator), not the code.
3. **v3 against v2 (`main`): no site moves.** `veg_ba_ha`, `veg_n_plots` and `veg_type` are identical
   at all 41 sites with a bundle. The admitted, absence and excluded event totals are identical
   (3892, 493, 1729). Only exclusion labels move: the sibling now labels 14 identity conflicts, 12
   presence conflicts and 4 missing areas itself (the Atlas labelled them `excl_vintage_*` or
   `excl_area_missing_or_le50` under v2), and the 46 `dataCollected=partial` holds plus 11 pre-2020
   partial events become 4 `excl_partial_hold` and 53 `excl_partial_hold_sibling_complete`.
   `veg_selected_vintage_plots` falls from 462 to 414 suite-wide because v3 labels 48 pre-2020
   selected events `held_metric_invalid` (admitted either way).
4. **v3 against the committed (legacy) family.** No type flips and no site goes from a finite value
   to NA. Every delta in that table is the legacy to plot-event change already sized in the v2 work,
   since v3 equals v2 here. Headline sites: STEI 17.944 to 18.012 m2/ha (+0.4%, 37 plots both);
   JERC 11.979 to 11.787 (-1.6%, 29 to 20 plots); OSBS 11.687 to 11.402 (-2.4%, 42 to 39 plots);
   SRER 5.092 to 4.773 (-6.3%, 38 plots both, shrubland); TALL 24.450 to 25.005 (+2.3%); GUAN 17.050
   to 17.855 (+4.7%, shrubland). The largest moves are SOAP (-18.8%, 36 to 31 plots), SJER (-12.7%)
   and KONZ (+10.6%, 6 to 5 plots).
5. **Sibling-complete partial events (V3-3).** 53 stand-channel events carry
   `excl_partial_hold_sibling_complete`: JERC 25, OSBS 25, YELL 3. Across both channels 54 plot events
   are marked complete by the sibling. Five of them (shrub channel) are held for an identity conflict
   and keep that more specific label.
6. **Row flags (V3-4).** Inside the selected events' stand snapshots, 0 rows carry
   `superseded_in_event` (the Atlas's latest-date rule already drops them) and 342 rows carry a
   `duplicate_of_uid`: BONA 315, TEAK 26, UKFS 1. The Atlas retains exact duplicate rows (R2), so these
   rows are in `veg_ba_ha` at those three sites today, under v2 as well.
